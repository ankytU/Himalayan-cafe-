About us

Himalayan Cafe began in 2015 as a tea stall with one stove. Today we are a small family restaurant, but we still make the dough, the achar and the masala by hand.

Our recipes come from the hills of Solukhumbu and the valleys of Kathmandu. We buy vegetables and meat from nearby farms, and we cook every dish when you order it.

Come in as a traveller. Leave as a regular.
